LetsPlay Design 2

Design 2: Playground

A second direction for LetsPlay that turns finding a game into a quick, physical interaction and makes progress visible.

  • Browse the list over the live map. Tap a game to see it on the map, tap + to join, tap a pin to find its card.
  • Tap a story ring for games starting soon.
  • Book: drag the time window along the day.
  • Squad: pick a player, then tap a fielding spot.
  • Me: your level, weekly goal, streak and badges.

Sample games, people and prices. Payments and messages are simulated.
